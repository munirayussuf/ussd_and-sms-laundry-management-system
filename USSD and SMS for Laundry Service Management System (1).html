USSD and SMS for Laundry Service Management System

Book laundry from any phone, no internet needed. Dial the code or send an SMS, the same way Safaricom services work. Works for formal laundries and for mama fua.

Try it

  • Formal laundry: dial *123#, press 1, then pick a laundry, a service and the kilos.
  • Mama fua: dial *123#, press 2, then pick your area and a mama fua.
  • SMS: open Messages and text HELP to 22123.
  • Provider side: in Messages, switch to Provider phone and text ACCEPT LD1001, READY LD1001 or DONE LD1001.
  • Press 0 on any screen to go back.
